# Route Project Display Policy

The application does not require or fabricate a minimum number of projects for a route.

Route results are source-backed:
- Verified government GIS geometry is eligible for exact corridor matching.
- Configured government work records retain their source/evidence status.
- OpenStreetMap construction is supplementary and is not treated as official government progress.
- PAIMANA portfolio records without project geometry may be shown as approximate monitoring candidates when a defensible place-name association exists.
- No random coordinates, demo projects, or simulated route records are added to reach a target count.

The complete 1,981-record PAIMANA registry remains the master portfolio dataset.
